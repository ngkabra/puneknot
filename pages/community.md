## What you get by joining

- **First go at tickets.** Talks are announced here before anywhere else, with a head start on registration.
- **Related Events.** A group where members post other in-person events in Pune worth going to: lectures, walks, performances, exhibitions.
- **General.** A group for discussion about any area of knowledge: science, technology, arts, humanities.

Joining the community puts you on the announcements list only. The two groups are optional; ask to join either from inside the community. An organizer approves each request, which can take a day or two. We moderate because of spammers, and we may message you first if we cannot tell that you are in Pune.

## Guidelines

- **Write it yourself.** No forwards.
- **Events go in Related Events,** and only in-person events in Pune. No webinars, no events elsewhere.
- **No politics, no religion.** You can discuss policy, but not politicians or parties. You can argue science against pseudoscience, but without attacking anyone's religion. Organizers will close a thread that turns partisan.
- **Be decent.** No personal attacks, nothing that makes others feel unwelcome.
- **If you see something off, tell an organizer.** If you are not sure whether a post belongs, ask one.
- **If the groups get too busy,** leave them and stay in the community. You will still get the announcements.
