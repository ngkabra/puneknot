## How it started

In November 2024, Ashish Kulkarni and Ravishankar Iyer discovered they lived near each other in Pune and shared a fondness for beer. They roped in Navin Kabra and Nagaraj Balasubramanian, met at a brewery, and by the end of the evening had a name. The first talk was on 27 March 2025: Prof. Pradeep Apte on the alchemy of brewing. It sold out, and most talks since have too. Harish Bhamidipati joined the organizers a few months later and brought the quiz with him.

The model is borrowed, with his blessing, from Anoop Mahajan's long-running Science on Tap. KnOT widens the brief from science to any area of knowledge where we can find someone who knows it well.

Pune KnOT is not a company or a registered body. Nobody is paid. The cover charge goes to the venue and comes back to you as food and drink.
