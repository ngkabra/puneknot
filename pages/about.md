## How it started

In early November 2024, Ravishankar Iyer was having much the same conversation with two people. With Nagaraj Balasubramanian, a cell biologist at IISER Pune, it was about starting something at Pagdandi like Chai and Why?, TIFR's public science talks. With Ashish Kulkarni, who lived nearby and shared his liking for beer, it was about hosting public talks over drinks.

The day after Ravishankar and Ashish spoke, by coincidence, Navin Kabra [tweeted](https://x.com/NGKabra/status/1854061279129833977) about Science on Tap, Anoop Mahajan's long-running series of talks by scientists in a Koregaon Park pub. He hoped someone would start one in Aundh or Baner, because for a true Punekar, Koregaon Park is "एवढं लांब?" (that far?).

That brought them all to a brewery in Balewadi on 14 November 2024, and they left with a name. Anoop gave it his blessing, and KnOT widened the brief from science to any subject. The first talk was on 27 March 2025, Prof. Pradeep Apte on the alchemy of brewing, and it sold out. Harish Bhamidipati joined the organizers a few months later and brought the quiz with him.

Pune KnOT is not a company or a registered body. Nobody is paid. The cover charge goes to the venue and comes back to you as food and drink.
