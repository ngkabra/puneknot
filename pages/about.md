## How it started

In early November 2024, Ashish Kulkarni and Ravishankar Iyer, who lived near each other and both liked beer, got talking about hosting public talks over drinks. The very next day, by coincidence, Navin Kabra [tweeted](https://x.com/NGKabra/status/1854061279129833977) about Science on Tap, Anoop Mahajan's long-running series of talks by scientists in a Koregaon Park pub. He hoped someone would start one in Aundh or Baner, because for a true Punekar, Koregaon Park is "एवढं लांब?" (that far?). That was enough for the three of them to fix an evening to meet.

In the days before that evening, Ravishankar found himself in a similar conversation with Nagaraj Balasubramanian, a cell biologist at IISER Pune, who had been thinking of starting something at Pagdandi like Chai and Why?, TIFR's public science talks. When Ravishankar told him what he and Ashish had in mind, Nagaraj liked the version with beer better, and came along.

They all met at a brewery in Balewadi on 14 November 2024 and left with a name. Anoop gave the idea his blessing, and KnOT widened the brief from science to any subject. The first talk followed on 27 March 2025, when Prof. Pradeep Apte spoke on the alchemy of brewing to a sold-out room. A few months later Harish Bhamidipati joined the organizers and brought the quiz with him.

Pune KnOT is not a company or a registered body. Nobody is paid. The cover charge goes to the venue and comes back to you as food and drink.
