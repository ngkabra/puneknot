Lyso-PS is a fat molecule that regulates communication in our brain and our immune system. Siddhesh talks about his lab's work on this tiny molecule with a big story, and how it could lead to significant health breakthroughs.

The talk is aimed at laypersons: you can (and should) come even if you have no background in any of these areas.

## About the speaker

Siddhesh Kamat is an Associate Professor in the Department of Biology at IISER Pune and a winner of the 2024 Infosys Prize. He completed his B.Tech. at UDCT, Mumbai and his Ph.D. in chemistry at Texas A&M University before joining IISER in 2016.
