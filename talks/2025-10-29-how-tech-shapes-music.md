How will AI impact music? Studying the past will reveal some answers. Music lover and internet gadabout Chuck looks at how past technologies have impacted music, its players and its economics. Part talk, part interactive quiz, and maybe some nice songs too.

## About the speaker

Chuck is a marketing consultant who is fond of overthinking how technology impacts culture. He has worked at ad agencies like Ogilvy and Leo Burnett and events firms OML and Insider, and has taught digital marketing at several places, including a couple of the IIMs, FLAME University and SIMC. He runs a content coaching program called The 6% Club, and has run podcasts and newsletters on topics from the origin of brands to irreverent humour, politics and technology.
