Does rebellion go against tradition in music, or is it an integral part of it? Is Indian classical music, as we hear it in concerts and on Instagram today, thriving or becoming irrelevant?

What kind of rebellion can save this art form from rigidity, nepotism, the reel economy, AI, and other challenges?

Mandar will share perspectives he has developed as a practicing musician and as a co-founder of Baithak Foundation, an organization that curates over 800 events every year and works with more than 200 young musicians.

## About the speaker

Mandar Karanjkar is a Hindustani classical vocalist and a composer who sets the poetry of saints like Kabir to music. In his previous avatar, he worked with almost 100 brands as a communications consultant.

He is a co-founder of Baithak Foundation.
