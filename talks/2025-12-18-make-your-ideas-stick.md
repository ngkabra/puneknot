Have you ever presented your ideas or findings only for the audience to ignore them, or forget them in a few minutes? Storytelling coach Ravi shares some timeless techniques to ensure that your ideas are heard, remembered and acted upon.

## About the speaker

Ravi helps leaders sharpen their storytelling skills. Since 2016 he has trained over 9,000 mid-to-senior leaders at companies such as Accenture, Flipkart, JP Morgan Chase, Shell and Unilever, and has guided over 150 nonprofits on crafting a better pitch. He writes a weekly newsletter and hosts a podcast on storytelling. A Chartered Accountant and an IIM Ahmedabad MBA, he spent twelve years in finance, consulting, the social sector and a startup that tried to make history exciting for tourists (spoiler: it didn't work). He is the author of "Story Rules" (2025), and one of the co-founders of Pune KnOT.
