A year-end special, on a Wednesday. Vineet Alurkar and Dhruv Bhate read from their book,
*Rock 'n' Roll Dreams, Indian Reality: Two Friends. One Band. 123 Gigs.*, and play some of the
songs that inspired them and some numbers they wrote, accompanied by Bhupal and Kabir.

From the book:

> Growing up in the *khichdi* that is India, leaves little space for dreams. We don't mean the
> ordinary kind of dream, where you earn enough by your mid-30s to be in possession of a
> chauffeur-driven Mercedes and a Fair-and-Lovely backseat passenger. We mean the kind of dream
> that has always had little chance of success in India, and is, even at its most successful,
> financially underwhelming.
>
> Vineet and I had just such a dream – in a time when we were meant to be on our way to becoming
> engineers, doctors or lawyers, preferably then moving to some mostly-white country, we dreamt of
> becoming rock'n'roll stars in India.

## About the speakers

Vineet Alurkar is a singer-songwriter, musician and music instructor. He is most well known as the
founding member and front man of the bands The Blackbirds, Yoga Logik and The Baavdhan Booze Band.
He has been teaching music for over 20 years in Pune and is a visiting faculty member at several
schools in Europe. He lives in Pune, India, with his wife and daughter.

Dhruv Bhate is a Professor of Engineering at the Arizona State University and has spent half of
his life in India, and the other half in the US of A. He has a PhD in Mechanical Engineering and
spent ten years working industry jobs before becoming a faculty member. This is where he once
again found the time to dream, and write. He lives in Scottsdale, USA, with his wife and son.

Dhruv and Vineet first met each other in 1990, in a sixth-standard classroom in Pune, where they
began their 35-year, and ongoing, story of music, friendship and crazy dreams. This is their first
book.
